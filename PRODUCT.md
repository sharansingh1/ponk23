# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Priyanka Ghotra, turning 23, and the friend group celebrating her — both as the primary audience. Her friends are also contributors (each submitting a video, Drive link, or message for their own slot) and viewers of the finished site alongside her; this is a shared celebration piece the group experiences together, most likely pulled up on a big screen/TV at the party, not a private one-time reveal she opens alone. It must still hold up well on a phone for anyone browsing it afterward on their own.

## Product Purpose

A one-off tribute site for her 23rd birthday: a single continuous scroll journey that carries her friends' individual tributes (video, Drive link, or written message) inside a bold, cohesive Tequila Sunrise / golden-hour theme woven through with her specific interests. Success is her (and the room) feeling the craft and effort immediately — that this was made, not templated — and feeling celebrated, seen, and hyped for the year ahead.

## Positioning

The one thing every visitor should come away believing: her friends didn't just wish her happy birthday, they built her an experience — every detail, from the sun tracing the scroll to the finale, was chosen because it's specifically *her*.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: scroll all the way through the journey — every friend's tribute is worth stopping for.
- Secondary CTA: revisit/replay a specific friend's section or interactive waypoint (dice roll, wax seal, beat drop) after the first pass.
- The line a visitor should remember after 10 seconds: "this is unmistakably about her, not a generic template with her name swapped in."
- Belief ladder: (1) this took real, personal effort → (2) everyone actually came together for this → (3) I feel celebrated and loved.
- Proof on hand: the friends' own submitted photos/videos/messages *are* the proof — no separate testimonial section needed; each tribute is presented as its own piece of evidence.

## Brand Personality

Golden-hour glamorous, warm, and a little theatrical — confident Leo energy, not shy. Bold enough to be memorable (interactive waypoints, a real narrative arc) while staying elegant and restrained in its base layer so friends' actual content never has to compete with the chrome around it. Playful without tipping into juvenile; premium without turning corporate or cold.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-template landing page: purple-to-blue gradients, Inter-everything, cards nested in cards, rounded-square icon tiles.
- Literal Hawaiian/tiki/luau imagery (palm-leaf print, tiki masks) — the brief is sunset-cocktail-hour, explicitly not this.
- Childish/cartoonish Disney treatment (literal Mickey ears, kiddie fonts) — she's a "Disney adult," not a toddler's birthday.
- Flat corporate finance-dashboard look for the "CEO Era" waypoint — that section should read as glamorous and celebratory, never like an actual accounting tool or a joke at her job's expense.
- A cluttered mood-board mess where five fandoms visually fight each other — every interest waypoint must still read as the same site, on the same road.

## Design Principles

- **One road, many chapters**: every fandom reference is a waypoint on the single sunset-to-sunrise scroll journey, never a competing sub-theme — this is what keeps five fandoms cohesive.
- **Chrome stays quiet, content stays loud**: the friend "stage" card frame is deliberately restrained and consistent so each friend's actual tribute is always the visual focus, even while surrounding waypoints go bold.
- **Bold means interactive, not busy**: interest references express themselves through one strong interaction each (roll a d20, break a wax seal, drop the beat) rather than decorative density.
- **The arc carries the meaning**: sunset → night (the party/journey) → sunrise (the finale) mirrors both the drink's name and "rising into a new year" — motion and color always serve that story, not decoration for its own sake.
- **Premium is restraint plus one flawless signature move**: physics-based easing everywhere, but the scroll-linked sun/path animation is the one hero effect everything else supports.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No reduced-motion fallback required per explicit decision — full animation for all visitors regardless of system motion preference. Standard responsive support for both desktop (primary target: shared big-screen viewing at the party) and mobile. The site should be unlisted (noindex, not submitted to search engines, reachable only via direct link) given it contains friends' personal photos/videos of her; no login/passcode gate needed.
